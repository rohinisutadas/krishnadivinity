Terms & Refunds

Terms and conditions governing course registration, digital access, and cancellations for Krishna Divinity.

1. Course Access & Delivery

Course access is intended exclusively for the registered student. Zoom links for live sessions and access to digital study materials are delivered electronically via WhatsApp and email upon registration and payment confirmation.

2. Gita Foundation Course

The ₹108 registration fee covers full access to scheduled live online classes, session recordings, student login access, and digital study resources as described on the course page.

3. Practical AI Course

The ₹108 registration covers the 10-module live online AI training. Complete live plus recorded access is available for ₹1,080. Students registered for the live modules can upgrade to complete access for ₹972 at any time during the course.

4. Cancellation & Refund Policy

We want every student to have a positive learning experience. If you are unable to attend the scheduled batch, you may request a full refund by contacting us prior to the start of the first live class. Once classes commence, registration fees are non-refundable; however, you may request a free transfer to a future batch. Approved refunds will be credited back to your original payment method (via Razorpay) within 5–7 business days.

5. Contact & Support

For any inquiries regarding registration, batch transfers, or refunds, please reach out via our official WhatsApp community or support channels.
