Privacy Policy

Draft for the registration service. Support contact and retention arrangements must be finalised before public registrations open.

Registration information

The form asks for your name, WhatsApp number with country code, email address, city and selected course. Date of birth is optional. We record your course-update consent, registration time, batch assignment and registration status.

How information is used

These details are used to administer your selected course, organise batches and communicate course-related updates. The registration consent does not subscribe you to promotional messages.

Storage and access

When the registration service is enabled, submissions pass through our website service hosted on Netlify and are stored in a restricted Google Sheets workbook through Google Apps Script. Only authorised course administrators should have access. The browser keeps a registration reference and status for the current session, not your name, phone number or date of birth.

Payments

No payments are collected by this registration form. Payment and course access will be arranged separately.

Corrections and contact

The official support contact and record-retention arrangements will be published before registration opens.
