Privacy Policy

At Krishna Divinity, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how your personal information is collected, used, and stored.

1. Information We Collect

When you register for a course, we collect your full name, WhatsApp phone number (with country code), email address, city, and selected course. Providing date of birth is optional. We also record your consent for receiving course-related updates and batch assignment details.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your details are used solely to administer your selected courses, allocate batch slots, provide Zoom session links, deliver study resources, and send essential course notifications. We do not sell, rent, or share your contact information with third parties for commercial advertising.

3. Payment Information

Course fees are processed securely via our trusted payment gateway partner, Razorpay. Krishna Divinity does not capture, process, or store your credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, net banking credentials, or UPI PINs. All online transactions comply with industry-standard PCI-DSS encryption protocols.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your registration records are handled securely through encrypted connections and stored in restricted administrative records managed via Google Apps Script and secure serverless functions. Only authorized course administrators have access to student records.

5. Contact and Inquiries

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, wish to update your details, or request deletion of your information, please connect with us through our official WhatsApp community or support channels.
